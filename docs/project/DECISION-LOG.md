| DEC-019 | 2026-10-10 | **Editable wedge matrix for partial-swing carry distances and Caddie decisions** | Provide a wedge matrix in Bag / Club Distances for each configured wedge (e.g. PW, GW, SW, LW), with editable distance references for quarter, half, three-quarter and full swings. Swing fractions are initial configurable categories, not a claim of mechanically exact swing length. Distinguish carry from total distance, player-entered reference distances from observed tracked shots, and show sample size/uncertainty before using measured values to inform the Caddie. Capture swing variant with each wedge shot; compare alternatives at the same target distance when evidence supports it. Keep punch/knockdown shots separately identifiable rather than folding them into full-swing or wedge-fraction averages. Never invent partial-shot yardages from full-swing distances. | Accepted |
| DEC-018 | 2026-10-10 | **Club statistics show editable short, long and average distances, including partial wedge and punch-shot variants** | For every club in the player's bag, club stats should show the shortest, longest and average recorded shot distances. These values must be editable by the player, including later corrections or manual entry; edits must not silently rewrite the underlying shot history. Shot capture must support partial/controlled wedge shots and punch shots as identifiable shot types or variants, so they can be recorded and analysed separately from full-swing shots instead of distorting the club's normal-distance picture. Preserve shot context and distinguish observed data from player-edited club reference values. | Accepted |
| DEC-017 | 2026-10-10 | **UiDo Caddie uses a recommendation → commitment → pre-round handshake → on-course execution → measured result → improved recommendation loop** | Insights should identify a meaningful player pattern, propose a specific test for the next round, and—when the player accepts it—lock it in as an active experiment. At the pre-round handshake, briefly ask when the golfer last played and whether they have practised since, then remind them of the agreed experiment. Avoid repeating known, current answers; let the golfer skip or change the experiment. During play, surface the agreed experiment only in relevant situations (for example, testing one club more at suitable 7i/8i approach distances when short misses are frequent). Record shot context and outcomes, then compare like-for-like results after the round and retain, revise or discard the recommendation based on sufficient evidence. Show the relevant club/distance sample and uncertainty; do not infer a club change from an overall miss rate alone. | Accepted |

## Trigger design requirements
Document initiating actor/event, golfer need, preconditions/permissions, connectivity/offline behaviour, idempotency/retries, failure/cancellation, test isolation, and evidence for each live trigger.
