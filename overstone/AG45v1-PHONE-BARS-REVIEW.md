## Layout implemented

- Top bar spans the full screen width and is 8% of screen height.
- Right-hand area retains the same 60%/40% horizontal split and starts below the top bar, continuing to the bottom edge.
- Bottom bar is 8% of screen height and spans from the left edge to the right-hand area's left edge.
- Bars meet directly, with no intentional gaps.
- The geometry viewport occupies the remaining 84% height.
- The viewport height and SVG viewBox are adjusted together so the existing route projection and fit logic continue to use the available viewport dimensions.
